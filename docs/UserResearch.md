## Observation 1:
### Observation Statement: 
The effort that it takes to setup an app like PackReady is a top concern amongst the percentage of users that
took the survey.
### Supporting Data:
Out of 31 answers to our listed concerns question,
19 responses included "Takes too much effort to set up" as part of their selection.
### Team Action (if any):
When approaching our design language and golden path to our app, a top priority when creating the foundation is to make sure that there is
clear ease of access and use to our app. The main purpose should be easily executable, and it should be clear what does what without a long line of buttons and pages.
