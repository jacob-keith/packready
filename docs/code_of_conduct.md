## Team Policies

### Technology Use
In keeping with the 305Soft policy, there will be no distracted use of technology during any team meeting. Phones and other devices must be put away.

### Decisions
The team will discuss each decision together. If everyone agrees, we move forward. If we cannot agree, we will decide by vote.

### Attendance
The team communicates through our group text and our meetings. Members are generally expected to attend every meeting. If a member cannot attend, they should post in the group chat. Each week, the Scrum Master will check in to make sure everyone has completed their work. If 2 or more members fail to show up to a meeting then a remote meeting shall be held no more than two days after the class meeting.

### Assignments
At the beginning of each sprint, the team will divide up duties. Throughout the sprint, we will hold brief check-ups to track progress.

### Participation
We will make sure everyone stays engaged and contributes during meetings, and we will keep each other informed through the group chat.

### Meeting Times and Locations
The team meets in class on Mondays and holds a brief meeting through the group chat. An additional meeting time is to be announced.

### Agenda and Minutes
Agendas and meeting notes will be kept and shared in Google Docs.

### Promptness
Meeting material will be written down and shared so that anyone who arrives late can be briefed. Members should be available to answer questions, and anyone who expects to be late should communicate it ahead of time.

### Conversational Courtesies
Members will be polite, listen to everyone, and help any teammate who is struggling whenever they can.

### Enforcement and Feedback
Team members will check in on one another to make sure these guidelines are being followed.
